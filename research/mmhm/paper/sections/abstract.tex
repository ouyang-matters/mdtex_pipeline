\begin{abstract}
We maintain the Betti numbers over $\Z$ of a simplicial complex of dimension at
most three under insertions and deletions of simplices. Version~1 of this paper
proposed a localised update of a boundary matrix reduced by discrete Morse
theory; that update is not exact, and this version replaces it. For each
dimension we maintain a reduced matrix $R=BV$ together with the change of
basis~$V$. An inserted simplex is reduced against the existing pivots; a
deleted simplex is removed from every basis chain that contains it, by adding
either a cycle or the column with the smallest pivot. This deletion step is the
rank-only specialisation of known updates for zigzag persistence and
warm-started reductions. We prove that the Betti numbers are exact after every
edit, for every order of the cells. Discrete Morse theory enters only through
that order: if cells are ordered by reverse removal time in a collapse, every
gradient pair is an apparent pair, so the reduction performed at a rebuild makes
column additions only in the columns of critical cells, where, after following
the gradient flow, it performs exactly the additions of the reduction of the
Morse complex. The cost of an edit is output-sensitive; we
prove that it admits no bound in terms of the size of the edit, and we report
footprints and reduction-chain lengths as measurements. We evaluate the method
on five workload families, with complexes of up to \NumSizeMax{} simplices,
against static recomputation, including GUDHI, and
against ablations of the order and of the rebuild policy, and we check every
exact method against three independent oracles.
